# Dashboard Warning Lights

## Check Engine light (amber)
Steady amber: a fault was detected in the engine or emissions system. Often
non-urgent (e.g. loose fuel cap, a sensor), but have it scanned soon.
Flashing: a serious misfire — reduce speed and get it inspected right away to
avoid catalytic converter damage.

## Oil Pressure light (red)
Red oil-can symbol: low oil pressure. This is serious — stop safely, switch off
the engine, and check the oil level. Driving with low oil pressure can destroy
the engine. Do not ignore.

## Battery / Charging light (red)
The charging system is not keeping the battery charged (often the alternator or
belt). You may lose electrical power. Get it checked promptly.

## Coolant Temperature light (red)
The engine is overheating. Stop safely, switch off, and let it cool. Do not open
the radiator cap while hot. Continuing to drive can warp the cylinder head.

## Brake System light (red)
Could be a low brake fluid level, a fault, or simply the handbrake left on. If it
stays on with the handbrake released, treat brakes as unsafe and inspect.

## ABS light (amber)
The anti-lock braking system has a fault. Normal braking usually still works, but
anti-lock function may not. Get it inspected — it is a safety system.

## Airbag / SRS light (amber)
A fault in the airbag system. Airbags may not deploy in a crash. This is a safety
system — have it diagnosed.

## TPMS (tyre pressure) light (amber)
One or more tyres is low on pressure. Check and inflate to the correct pressure.

## Severity summary
Red lights generally mean stop or act now. Amber lights mean investigate soon.
Anything involving brakes, steering, or airbags is safety-critical — recommend a
physical inspection rather than guessing.
