# Common Symptoms and Likely Causes

These are general possibilities, not a diagnosis. Anything affecting brakes,
steering, or airbags needs a physical inspection.

## Squealing or squeaking when braking
Most commonly worn brake pads — many pads have a wear indicator that squeals on
purpose to tell you it is time to replace them. Could also be dust or glazing.
Recommend a brake inspection.

## Grinding when braking
Often the brake pads are worn through to metal, contacting the disc. This can
damage the discs. Recommend stopping heavy braking and getting an urgent brake
inspection.

## Vibration through the steering wheel when braking
Frequently warped brake discs, or wheel-balance issues. Recommend a brake and
wheel inspection.

## Car pulls to one side
Possible wheel alignment, uneven tyre pressure, or a brake issue. Recommend an
alignment check.

## White / blue / black exhaust smoke
White: often coolant burning (possible head gasket) or just condensation on a
cold start. Blue: burning oil. Black: running rich (fuel/air issue). Recommend an
engine inspection if it persists.

## Engine overheating
Low coolant, a failing thermostat, a leak, or a faulty radiator fan. Stop safely
and let it cool; do not keep driving hot.

## Battery keeps going flat
An old battery, a parasitic drain, or a charging (alternator) fault. Recommend a
battery and charging-system test.

## Rough idling or stalling
Could be spark plugs, air/fuel delivery, or a sensor. Recommend a diagnostic scan.

## Knocking noise from suspension over bumps
Possible worn suspension bushes, links, or shock absorbers. Recommend a
suspension inspection.

## Burning smell
Could be brakes overheating, an oil leak on a hot surface, or an electrical
fault. Electrical burning smells should be treated seriously — inspect promptly.
