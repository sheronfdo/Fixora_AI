# Service Intervals

## Engine oil change
Most petrol cars: every 5,000 km or 6 months, whichever comes first.
Diesel engines: every 5,000–7,500 km. Hybrids: every 8,000–10,000 km.
Always change the oil filter with the oil. Use the oil grade your manufacturer
specifies; using the wrong grade reduces engine protection.

## Oil grade guidance
Mineral oil: budget option, change more often (~5,000 km).
Semi-synthetic: good balance for most cars.
Full synthetic: better protection and longer intervals, common for newer engines.
Premium/fully synthetic: high-performance and luxury vehicles.

## Brake pads and discs
Inspect brake pads every 10,000 km. Front pads typically last 30,000–50,000 km
depending on driving. Squealing or grinding means inspect immediately.
Brake fluid should be replaced roughly every 2 years.

## Transmission fluid
Automatic: check every 40,000 km, replace per manufacturer schedule
(often 60,000–100,000 km). CVT fluid: follow the manufacturer interval closely —
CVTs are sensitive to the correct fluid. Manual gearbox oil: ~60,000 km.

## Coolant
Replace engine coolant every 40,000 km or 2 years. Never open the radiator cap
when the engine is hot.

## Tyres
Rotate every 10,000 km. Replace when tread depth is below 1.6 mm. Check tyre
pressure monthly. Uneven wear can indicate alignment or suspension issues.

## Air and cabin filters
Engine air filter: every 15,000–20,000 km (sooner in dusty conditions).
Cabin/pollen filter: every 15,000 km or once a year.

## Battery
A typical car battery lasts 3–5 years. Weak cranking, dim lights, or needing
frequent jump-starts suggest the battery is failing.
