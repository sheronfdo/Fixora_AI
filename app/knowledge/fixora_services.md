# Fixora Services and Booking

## What Fixora offers
Fixora lets you book vehicle service and repair appointments, see an estimated
cost before you book, track your vehicle's progress live, and keep a digital
maintenance history. All prices are in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR).

## Service packages
Common services include Oil Change, Brake Repair, Full Inspection, and Body Wash.
The exact list and base prices are managed by the service centre and shown in the
app when you book.

## How the cost estimate works
The estimate is calculated from your vehicle type (standard vs luxury), engine
type and transmission, current mileage, the chosen oil grade, brake condition,
and the service's base charge. Higher mileage and luxury vehicles cost more. The
estimate is indicative — the final bill is confirmed after inspection, and any
additional parts or labour are itemised.

## How to book
1. Add your vehicle to My Garage (registration, make, model, year, engine, mileage).
2. Open Book Service, pick the vehicle, service, date and an available time slot.
3. Review the estimated cost and confirm.
Each time slot has limited capacity, so full slots are shown as unavailable.

## Tracking your service
After drop-off you can track live status: Vehicle Received, Inspection Started,
Service In Progress, Quality Checking, and Ready For Collection. You get a
notification at each step, so you do not need to call the centre.

## Payment
When the work is done the centre issues an invoice with the original estimate
plus any additional parts and labour. You can view and pay it in the app.

## Cancelling
You can cancel a booking from your history while it is still Pending or Accepted.

## Vehicle condition and value
Fixora can estimate your vehicle's condition percentage and an approximate market
value range based on its service history, age, mileage and maintenance. It is an
informative estimate, not a certified valuation.
